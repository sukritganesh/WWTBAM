# Geography set authoring notes

## Scope

Release 001 v1.1.0 adds three Geography-only curated sets in `curated-007-geography-collection.json`:

| Set | Emphasis | Required countries-and-capitals question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical Geography: Forces and Features | Rivers, mountain systems, seas, landforms, drainage, and tectonics | Level 4: Quito, Ecuador, in the Andes |
| Borders and Human Geography | Borders, languages, exclaves, enclaves, and unusual territorial relationships | Level 5: Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso |
| Islands, Seas, and Archipelagos | Major islands, straits, archipelagos, maritime regions, and remote territories | Level 8: Ngerulmud, Palau |

All 45 questions are separate from the Fresh Mix pool. Each set has exactly one question at every level from 1 through 15 and exactly one question tagged `capitals`.

## Difficulty model

- Levels 1–5 use widely recognizable places and one-step associations. Distractors come from the same broad type without relying on trick wording.
- Levels 6–10 require intermediate regional knowledge, such as estuaries, exclaves, straits, and less familiar island relationships.
- Levels 11–14 introduce specialist but nameable geographic concepts and locations. Hints provide a genuine route to deduction without repeating the answer.
- Level 15 uses precise, stable expert knowledge: a three-way continental watershed, doubly landlocked states, and a remote French subantarctic territory.

Difficulty is based on the obscurity and relational depth of the geographic fact, not on spelling traps, disputed sovereignty, transient rankings, or deliberately confusing answer choices.

## Editorial safeguards

- Existing Geography prompts and their central facts were reviewed before authoring; the supplement avoids duplicating the original set and pool topics.
- Questions use four distinct, credible choices, one stable correct reference, a non-answer-leaking hint, and a concise explanation.
- The pack declares zero time-sensitive questions and avoids population totals, current officeholders, active conflicts, and unsettled border claims.
- Automated validation checks IDs, prompts, choices, category membership, exact level order, reciprocal set references, coverage totals, and source hashes.
- The content remains `assistant-reviewed-draft`; human factual review and player-based difficulty calibration are still recommended before editorial certification.
